# Coreside Marketing Claim Ledger

This ledger establishes the boundary between verified engineering reality in the Coreside repository, approved marketing language for the launch website, and excluded overclaims that must not appear in copy.

| Topic | Proposed Public Statement | Supporting Repo Code / Document or Vision | Approved Language | Excluded Overclaim |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Product Category & Name** | "Coreside is a personal software environment." | Exact product name: Coreside (`docs/PRODUCT_LANGUAGE_GUIDE.md`, `README.md`). Tauri 2 desktop shell, React/TS, Rust core, SQLite local storage. | "Coreside is an AI-native personal software workspace for desktop." | Do not call it "Coreside AI", "Core-Side", or an "external website builder / full-stack SaaS generator". |
| **Apps vs Tools** | "Build apps right inside your workspace." | `docs/PRODUCT_LANGUAGE_GUIDE.md` mandates consumer term **"Apps"** instead of "tools". Internal IDs use `toolId` / `ToolRenderer.tsx`, but public UI uses "Apps". | "Build personal apps right inside your workspace, then keep changing them as your needs change." | Do not market "mini-tools" or technical jargon like "declarative AST nodes". |
| **Evolution & State Preservation** | "Apps evolve through conversation while keeping your data." | E2E journeys (`e2e/specs/22-application-evolution.spec.ts`, `31-multi-surface-progressive-evolution.spec.ts`, `37-cross-surface-reactive-sync.spec.ts`). `ApplicationPlan` protocol with `kind: "evolve"` and data model preservation. | "Keep what you build. Change what you need. When you ask for updates, your existing data and tasks stay intact." | Do not claim "never lose any data under any circumstance" or "infinite zero-bug undo". State preservation is an intentional architectural goal. |
| **Security & Approval Boundary** | "You review and approve changes before they are applied." | `src/components/chat/ChangeProposalCard.tsx`, `docs/SECURITY.md`, `docs/GENERATED_APPLICATION_RUNTIME.md`. Registered action gateway (`execute_registered_action`), approval tokens, single entry point. | "See what changes before you apply them. Structured app definitions run in a protected desktop core with user review." | Do not claim "100% unhackable", "SOC 2 certified", "military-grade security", or "zero data ever leaves your computer". |
| **Local Data Persistence** | "Your apps and conversations are saved locally on your computer." | Local SQLite database via Tauri Rust core (`docs/SECURITY.md`, `src-tauri/src/db`). | "Your conversations, personal apps, and project data are stored locally on your device." | Do not claim cloud sync is live or multi-device automatic sync is available today. |
| **Automations** | "Automate routines inside your workspace." | `docs/AUTOMATIONS.md`, `src/components/automations/AutomationsPanel.tsx`. Automations run **only while Coreside is open**. Minimum 5m interval, max 1 catch-up on reopen. | "Set recurring routines and reminders that run right while Coreside is open on your desktop." | Do not claim "24/7 background cloud bots" or "autonomous tasks running while your laptop is closed". |
| **Web Research** | "Search and cite information from the web." | `docs/ROADMAP.md`, `docs/SECURITY.md`. Crawl4AI local sidecar and Exa search integration with URL validation and SSRF protection. | "Research topics and reference web content directly within your workspace." | Do not claim unfiltered, unrestricted live browsing or autonomous web agent purchasing. |
| **Audience & Use Cases** | "Built for students, creators, and independent workers." | Verified app recipes in repository: Study Planner, Habit Tracker, Checklists, Quiz & Flashcards, Custom Dashboards. | "From semester study schedules to daily habit tracking and custom personal dashboards." | Do not claim enterprise ERP, financial trading execution, medical diagnostic workflows, or multi-tenant team SaaS. |
| **Release Readiness & Availability** | "Join the early-access waitlist." | Repository state is pre-launch/in-development. Packaged launch smoke verified, public general release not yet shipped. | "Early access is coming. Join the waitlist for updates." | Do not fabricate release dates (e.g., "Launching November 1st"), fake user counts ("Join 50,000 users"), or fake testimonials/investors. |
| **Platform Support** | "Available for desktop." | Tauri desktop target (macOS packaged adaptive icon in `docs/BRANDING.md`, multi-platform desktop architecture). | "Desktop-first personal software. Supported platforms will be announced as early access opens." | Do not guarantee unverified Windows/Linux builds or publish fake iOS/Android app store badges. |
